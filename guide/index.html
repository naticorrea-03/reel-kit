Claude Code skills for talking-head reels

Reel Kit

Turn a long interview or talking-head video into finished 9:16 reels in your brand. It picks the hot takes, cuts a tight script, adds graphics on the right words, and checks every frame before you watch.

Every demo in this guide uses an AI-generated person made with Higgsfield (Kling 3.0). She is not a real person.

How to use it

  1. Install

    Clone the repo and run the installer. It links five skills into Claude Code and checks ffmpeg, Node, Python and the face detector.

    git clone https://github.com/naticorrea-03/reel-kit ~/reel-kit
cd ~/reel-kit && ./install.sh

  2. Set up your brand

    One interview, once. Your colors, fonts, logo, look, caption style and the things you never want to see. Saved to ~/.reel-kit/profile.json.

    /reel-setup

  3. Make reels

    Point it at a long video. It reads the whole transcript, picks the hot takes, writes each clip as a script, plans one graphic per beat, cuts, renders and masters.

    /reel-edit ~/Downloads/interview.mov

  4. Check before you watch

    Layout audit on every sixth of a second, words check, loudness, and beat sheets Claude reads itself. Errors block the render.

    /reel-qa

  5. Review and give notes

    Your reels land on a review page in your brand. Approve or redo each one, pin a note to the exact second, or comment on one storyboard beat. Claude reads every note before the next round. It can be a private claude.ai page you share with a client, or a page on your Mac.

    /reel-review

  6. Browse effects

    Ask for what you need in plain words. "Add a popup when she says follow me." It picks from the library below.

    /reel-effects

The rules it follows

Each one came from a real round of review notes. The full story is in docs/LEARNINGS.md.

  • Hot takes, not topics. A clip is one sharp claim and the line that pays it off. Length follows the thought, 20 or 90 seconds.
  • Every graphic adds something. A note that repeats the caption is refused by the build. No sparkles, no glows, no filler words behind the head.
  • Show the noun on the word. Named company, real logo. Named paper, real screenshot. It lands on the frame the word is said.
  • Sounds are literal. Cash register on money, shutter on a photo, silence otherwise.
  • Nothing covers a face. Placement reads the cutout and the face boxes. The audit blocks anything that slips through.
  • Fix the class, not the instance. Every miss becomes a guard or an audit rule, tested on a broken clip.
